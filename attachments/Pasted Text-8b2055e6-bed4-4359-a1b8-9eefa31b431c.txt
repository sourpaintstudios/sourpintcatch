CATCH — First-Person Mobile Catch Game
Core loop: One player, one ball. The phone is the glove and the ball. Hold a button, swing your arm, release the button — the ball flies. Tilt the phone to catch it. Haptic buzz, leather snap, and dust puff all fire on the same frame when you catch it.
Physics model: The throw always goes toward a fixed receiver position (auto-aimed). Swing strength sets power and arc quality, not direction. Clean swings produce flat, accurate arcs. Jerky swings produce high pops or wobble that force last-second tilt adjustments. No free-aim. No multiplayer yet.
Throw parameters:
Gentle toss: ~1g peak, ~25–35 mph, short arc
Normal catch: ~1.5–2g, ~40–50 mph, ~1.5s hang time, ~80 ft range
Hard throw: ~2–3g, ~60–70 mph
Dead zone below 0.6g (ignore hand tremor)
Gesture duration: 0.15–0.8 seconds (too fast = noise, too slow = not a throw)
Launch angle from phone tilt at button release, but constrained so the ball still arrives near the receiver
Wobble amplitude scales with swing jerkiness (smooth = tight spiral, jerky = duck)
Catch:
Glove = sphere ~0.25 m radius around phone position (gyroscope-driven)
Catch fires when ball enters radius and closing speed is positive
Auto-lock: when ball is within 0.5 m, glove snaps toward it
Web Gem: catch within ~0.08 m of glove center = bonus + streak multiplier
Haptic: 80–120 ms heavy pulse, intensity scales with ball speed
Audio: release swoosh → flight whistle with Doppler build → crisp leather snap on catch
Visual: dust puff off glove leather on impact, light burst on Web Gem
Start with baseballs only. One ball type, one clean arc, one snap. Footballs and wobble mechanics come later.
Engine: Unity. Personal tier is free. Use built-in physics for gravity and drag. No custom physics solver needed for prototype.
What to build first:
Phone motion input: accelerometer peak for speed, gyroscope for catch position, button-hold for charge
Ball projectile: parabolic arc with gravity and simple drag, auto-aimed at receiver
Catch detection: proximity check + auto-lock
Feedback: haptic + snap sound + dust particle, all on the same frame
Solo mode: throw, catch, streak counter
Do not build yet: multiplayer, football spiral physics, menus, progression systems, or anything that isn't the core throw-catch loop.
